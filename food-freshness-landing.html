What the platform does

Every module tied to one job: keep food out of the bin.

From the moment a product image is uploaded to the moment a storage recommendation is issued — one continuous pipeline.

01 · Vision

Image Analysis Engine

Detects color degradation, texture change, mold, bruising, and physical damage from an uploaded food photo.

02 · Scoring

Freshness Assessment

Combines visual condition, storage data, and product age into a single weighted freshness score and category.

03 · Forecast

Shelf-Life Prediction

Estimates remaining shelf life and expiry risk from image data, storage temperature, humidity, and packaging type.

04 · Environment

Storage Monitoring

Tracks temperature, humidity, and air circulation against compliance limits and surfaces optimization tips.

05 · Action

Recommendation Engine

Suggests storage fixes, consumption order, and inventory rotation to cut waste before it happens.

06 · Insight

Dashboards & Analytics

Role-specific views — consumer, retail, warehouse, admin — over freshness trends, alerts, and reports.

Access by role

One platform, five different jobs to do.

Consumer

Checks freshness reports and shelf-life on their own food inventory at home.

Retail Manager

Watches inventory-wide quality analytics and shelf-life alerts across the store.

Warehouse Operator

Monitors storage compliance, batch freshness, and environmental conditions.

Food Quality Inspector

Validates spoilage detections and reviews compliance against food safety standards.

Administrator

Manages users, system health, and platform-wide reporting configuration.
