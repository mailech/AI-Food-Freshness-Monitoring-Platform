DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Fresh Monitor, we prioritize your privacy. We collect minimal personal data to provide and improve our service, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and authentication details.
  • Usage & Food Data: Food images, nutritional tracking data, and freshness analysis query logs.
  • Technical Data: IP address, device type, and browser diagnostics for system security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and optimize the Fresh Monitor experience:

  • To generate AI analysis on food quality, shelf life, and macronutrient content.
  • To manage your account, authenticate sign-ins, and process password resets.
  • To train and improve our internal AI freshness models using anonymized data.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your data is protected using end-to-end encryption standards both in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal data to third parties or advertising networks under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services

We may integrate trusted third-party providers for essential features such as authentication (e.g., Google, Apple) and transactional SMS/email services. These services adhere strictly to data protection privacy standards.

5. Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your personal data. You can request account deletion, access your stored data, or update your information at any time directly through your account settings or by contacting our support team.
